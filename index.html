01 Why we exist

It started with Aruana.

A few years ago, Ayana Bektassova was babysitting her younger cousin Aruana, who has developmental delays. One thing stayed with her: almost no one invited kids like Aruana to draw, to play, or to make something of their own.

They were treated — but rarely included.

So she started iZhureq with classmates at NIS IB Astana. The name comes from Kazakh: zhürek means heart — together, a brave heart. Today it is a student-led organization with five departments and a team of volunteers who show up for children every month.

Ayana Bektassova Founder, iZhureq

02 What we do

Art therapy that leaves every child with something they made.

We run creative sessions in hospitals and rehabilitation centers. Different art techniques help children develop fine motor skills, ease stress, and say what is hard to say out loud.

  1. 01

    Train first

    Before anyone works with children, our volunteers train with professional psychologists. Good intentions are not enough when a child is vulnerable.

  2. 02

    Make something

    Painting, handprints, paper fans, collage — simple techniques adapted to each child's age and condition.

  3. 03

    Draw & swap

    Every five minutes, drawings change hands. Each picture ends up being something we made together.

  4. 04

    Play & music

    Between rounds — games and music, so the room stays light and every child feels at ease.

03 Beyond hospital walls

City for Everyone

In 2025 we took iZhureq into the city itself. City for Everyone is a national competition where school teams design technical solutions that make urban spaces calmer, safer and more welcoming for children with autism spectrum disorder.

Who
Teams of 3–4 students, grades 7–8 and 9–11
Round 1
A two-minute video: the problem and a solution. 100+ teams, about 400 students from 10 cities
Final
24 teams pitched to a jury on 11 October 2025 at NIS IB Astana
Prizes
100,000 / 70,000 / 50,000 ₸ in each age category — to keep building the idea
04 Our journey

From one idea to a national organization.

  1. Nov 2024

    First art therapy session

    The National Center for Children's Rehabilitation welcomes our volunteers. Children paint, then play games to music between rounds.

  2. Feb 2025

    Recognized by the Ministry

    The Ministry of Enlightenment of Kazakhstan and its Department of Inclusive and Special Education award our founders letters of gratitude.

  3. Mar 2025

    The team grows

    We open recruitment for art, media and organization departments. Volunteers get real responsibility — and an official certificate for their work.

  4. Apr 2025

    Talking about autism

    A charity fair and a workshop for students, “ASD: main difficulties and stereotypes”, with art therapist and Orda methodologist Kuldraikhan Kanybekovna. 100% of the fair went to iZhureq.

  5. Jun 2025

    IB Global Youth Action Fund winner

    Selected from 1,100+ projects worldwide: a $2,500 grant, skill-building programs and the GYAF alumni community. Our work supports SDG 3 and SDG 10.

  6. Sep–Oct 2025

    City for Everyone

    Our first national competition: 100+ teams, about 400 students, and a final on 11 October.

05 The people

Founder

Ayana Bektassova

Ayana founded iZhureq after seeing how rarely children like her cousin were invited to create. She built the volunteer team, trained it with psychologists, brought art therapy into three medical and rehabilitation centers and launched City for Everyone.

“What I’m proudest of is not the numbers. It’s that for our volunteers, inclusion stopped being a word and became something they do.”

Built together with Nurgul, Kamila, Ayaulym, Akerke and volunteers across five departments. Thank you to our mentors Zhanagul Berikovna and Symbat Bolatbekkyzy, and to the psychologists of UMC Heart Center and NCCR who welcome us every time.
